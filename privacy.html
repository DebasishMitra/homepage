1. Introduction

Welcome to the Premium Homepage Dashboard. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how your data is collected, stored, and protected when you use our homepage tools, widgets, notebooks, and synchronizations.

2. Information We Collect

To provide a personalized, cloud-synchronized dashboard experience, we collect and process the following categories of information:

  • Authentication Information: Your secure email and encrypted password credentials managed by our cloud authentication partner (Supabase Auth).
  • User Profiles & Settings: Your interface configurations (such as dark mode state, glassmorphism panel selections, chosen theme colors, custom background images, and custom time formats).
  • Productivity Data: All private information entered into dashboard tools including your synchronized cloud Bookmarks, private Notepad notes, and custom Web Links.
  • Geolocation & Network Information: Real-time network details such as your approximate location (City/Region/Country) and IPv4 address are processed client-side via public geo-mapping APIs to load your local weather reports and system network cards. We do not store your physical locations or log history on our database.

3. How Data is Stored and Protected

We rely on high-grade cloud services and native client-side storage to protect your private records:

  • Cloud Database Security: All user profiles, bookmarks, links, and notepad entries are securely stored on our cloud instance, isolated by strict PostgreSQL **Row Level Security (RLS)** policies. This guarantees that your private database records can only be read, modified, or accessed by your verified authenticated session.
  • Local Browser Storage: Temporary settings, local fallbacks, and rate-limiting attempt registers are held securely on your device's browser using `localStorage`.
  • Brute Force & Rate Limit Defenses: Login and signup forms enforce a rigid, browser-resilient rate limiter (maximum 5 attempts per minute) to defend your account credentials from automated brute-force attacks.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or lease your private data to third-party advertisers or brokers. Information is only shared with standard cloud service providers necessary to operate the service:

  • Supabase API: Runs authentication verification, securely holds encrypted passwords, and powers data tables.
  • Open-Meteo API: Approximate geocoding and coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to dynamically generate local weather forecasts without saving persistent location histories.
  • Vercel Speed Insights: Dynamically measures page loading performance to optimize site speed and layout stability.

5. Your Rights and Deleting Your Data

You retain full authority over your data. You have the right to request, correct, or permanently wipe your records at any time:

  • Data Purge (Account Deletion): You can permanently wipe your account at any moment. Simply go to the **Settings > Security** tab and click **Delete Account**. This safely invokes a secure Postgres cascade deletion that completely deletes your authentication record and instantly wipes all matching bookmarks, notes, and settings.

6. Contact & Inquiries

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, your database records, or security defenses, please feel free to reach out to the project administrator.
